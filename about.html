Our Story

About GIFT Titanium

Founded in 2018, GIFT Titanium has spent 8 years mastering pure titanium drinkware—now bringing that expertise to global B2B partners worldwide.

8 Years of Pure Titanium Expertise

GIFT Titanium (赐贤) was founded in 2018 with a clear focus: master pure titanium drinkware for the Chinese market and do it better than anyone else. For 8 years, we built our manufacturing capabilities, refined our processes, and grew our product line to 25 models with 100+ SKUs.

Today, we are taking that same expertise global. Our products are manufactured in Yongkang, Zhejiang—the industrial hub behind some of the world's most recognized drinkware brands—using only Ti>99.8% aerospace-grade pure titanium, FDA and LFGB certified for international markets.

"8 years building the best titanium drinkware in China. Now we're ready to share it with the world."

We partner with global brands, gift wholesalers, outdoor gear distributors, and corporate gift suppliers who demand quality their customers can feel. With a minimum order of just 30 pieces and full OEM/ODM support, we make it easy to start and scale.

Design Patents & IP Protection

Our product designs are protected under Chinese intellectual property law.

GIFT Titanium develops proprietary drinkware forms in-house—not off-the-shelf copies. Multiple product lines are covered by registered design patents, giving B2B partners confidence that they source from the original designer and manufacturer, not a generic replica supplier.

For wholesale and OEM clients, this means clearer differentiation on shelf, stronger brand storytelling, and reduced risk when you build marketing around a distinctive titanium range.
