Frequently Asked Questions

Can I mix different models in one order?

Yes. You can combine multiple models in one order, as long as each model meets its individual MOQ (30 pcs). For example, 30 Goddess mugs + 30 Daphne mugs with the same logo engraving is a common order configuration.

How long does production take for a branded gift set?

Standard logo engraving on existing models: 15–20 business days after deposit and artwork approval. Custom gift box printing adds 5–7 days. Plan for 3–4 weeks total for most programs.

Can I see a sample before placing a bulk order?

Absolutely. We recommend ordering a branded sample first — production takes 5–7 days and includes your actual logo engraved on the product. Sample fee is refundable on bulk orders over 500 pcs.

What file format do I need for logo engraving?

Vector files only — AI, EPS, SVG, or high-resolution PDF. We'll send a digital mockup showing exactly how your logo will look on the product before production begins.
